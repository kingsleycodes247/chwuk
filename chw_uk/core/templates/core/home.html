About us

One community, rooted in tradition

The Community of HaShem Worldwide United Kingdom represent individuals and families from London and beyond who are dedicated to the Jewish faith, education, and communal living. Grounded in tradition and driven by shared values, our community strives to preserve Jewish heritage while making a positive impact both in London and across the globe.

Come and meet us

What we stand for

Our mission & values

1

Our Mission

Our mission is to propagate the SOUND DOCTRINE, which is the undiluted word of God throughout the whole world. We hope to achieve this through evangelism and media publications. We are heavily engaged in the production of materials to reach the unreached with the everlasting love of God.

2

Our Values

We are committed to nationwide media evangelism through television, radio, and print, delivering our core message, “Sound Doctrine,” to proclaim the Scriptures and teach the true tenets of the faith.
